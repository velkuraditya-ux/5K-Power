About 5K Power

Manufacturing Built Around Project Needs

Electrical equipment lead times, supply chain pressure, and inconsistent production can create significant risk for complex projects. 5K Power was established to provide greater control over manufacturing, technical standards, quality, and delivery.

Our operation brings manufacturing, procurement coordination, QA/QC, and engineered power solutions into a structured process designed to support reliable project execution.

5K Power currently supports the needs of Hays Electrical Services while developing the manufacturing platform and technical foundation for long-term growth.
